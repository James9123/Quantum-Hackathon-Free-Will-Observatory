Project title (placeholder)

Two qubits share an entangled state and are read out at four pairs of angles. Quantum theory predicts a combination of their correlations, S, up to 2√2 ≈ 2.83; every local hidden-variable model gives S ≤ 2. Run one job, turn the noise dial, and see how much noise it takes to wash the gap out.
