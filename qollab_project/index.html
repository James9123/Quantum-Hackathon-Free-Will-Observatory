The Free Will Observatory

We define free will here as the capacity to reshape possible futures. A two-qubit Self meets a World: an intention becomes a quantum phase, the World becomes a coupled system, and a perspective becomes a measurement choice. Each run submits one fresh simulator job of four probes and shows how the controls move a distribution of outcomes. The measurements are real outputs of the simulator; the Self and its philosophical meaning are our artistic construction.

The Observer changes the Perspective and reruns. Perspective changes the measured observable (the readout direction), not the prepared state, so the display probability moves; the two capacity probes keep that same readout and only move the intention, so Capacity stays comparable across perspectives.

Controls

The sliders edit a draft. Run experiment submits the whole draft as one job of four probes.

0 to π. Chooses the equatorial direction the Self is measured along.
